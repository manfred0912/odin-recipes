Description

There’s nothing like a juicy homemade cheeseburger with a juicy patty and fresh crisp toppings on a toasted bun, smothered with an irresistible burger sauce. Out of a deep love for hamburgers, I’ve tested tons of recipes over the years, and I’m convinced it doesn’t get any better than this recipe.

Ingredients

  • Burgir buns
  • Dill pickle slices
  • Fresh onions

  • Add the topics of your liking

Steps

How to Make the Best Hamburger Patties

  1. Use Ground Chuck Beef (80/20) - grind your own or buy it ground, but 20% fat is ideal, and keep it refrigerated until you’re ready to use it.
  2. Don’t overwork your meat – this will make it tough and dense.
  3. Shape the patties 1” wider than the bun since they shrink on the grill.
  4. Make an indentation in the center to prevent it from puffing up.
  5. Season at the last minute - Salt draws out liquid, changes the structure of proteins, and toughens burgers, so don’t season your beef until you have formed your patties and are ready to start grilling
  6. Get a good sear - Once on the grill, let patties brown and sear well (3-5 min) before flipping, and do not press down on the burger

How to Make Cheeseburgers

  1. Sauce - Apply a generous amount of sauce to both buns (see below for our favorite burger sauces).
  2. Toppings - Place pickles, lettuce, tomatoes, and red onion onto the bottom bun.
  3. Patty - Place cheesy burger patty over the veggies and cover with the top bun.
  4. Enjoy!
